White Water Rafting

Entendre's is designed to give you all the whitewater rafting information you'll need in order to book a whitewater rafting trip that best fits the rafting experience you and your friends are looking for.

History

Entendre's Whitewater has been in business since 1976 and are recognized as industry leaders in outdoor recreation. What started as a single raft and a passion for the river has grown into a premier adventure company.

Over the decades, we have guided hundreds of thousands of guests down North America's most exciting rivers, maintaining an unmatched commitment to safety, fun, and environmental stewardship.
